Normalized Numbers: E=not-all-zero
Subnormal Numbers: E=all-zero M=not-all-zero

qNaN(Quiet Not-a-Number): highest bit of M is 1
sNan(Signaling Not-a-Number): highest bit of M is 0

+Inf: S=0 E=all-one  M=all-zero     0 11111111 00000000000000000000000
-Inf: S=1 E=all-one  M=all-zero     1 11111111 00000000000000000000000
+0:   S=0 E=all-zero M=all-zero     0 00000000 00000000000000000000000
-0:   S=1 E=all-zero M=all-zero     1 00000000 00000000000000000000000
nan:      E=all-one  M=not-all-zero

NAN macro                           0 11111111 10000000000000000000000
qnan1:                              0 11111111 10000000000000000000001
qnan2:                              0 11111111 10000000000000000000010
sNaN                                0 11111111 01000000000000000000000
